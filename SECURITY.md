# Security policy

This policy applies to every public Aviometrics repository that doesn't have its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems **privately**:

1. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository: its **Security** tab, then
   **Report a vulnerability**.
2. If that isn't available, use the contact form on [aviometrics.com](https://aviometrics.com) and ask for a
   private channel. Don't include the details in the first message.

Please don't open a public issue or pull request for a vulnerability.

We'll acknowledge your report within five working days and keep you informed until it's resolved. Tell us whether
you'd like to be credited.

## Scope

In scope: our public repositories and the Bluetooth interface of ParaBaro devices as described in our published
specifications.

Out of scope: social engineering, physical attacks, denial of service, and findings in third-party services we
don't operate.
